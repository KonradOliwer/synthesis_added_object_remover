# Static methods and local functions of this module that mention no domain type (Ids or a Contracts type), each with its reason.
# Form: Type.Member | reason  (local function: Type.Method.Local). The test fails when an entry stops being flagged.
WhereRemovedRestorer.BuildPlans | Takes the step's own input record and gives its own plans record; the domain types sit inside both.
WhereRemovedRestorer.BuildLoop | Takes the step's own input and plans records and gives the step's loop; the domain types sit inside them.
WhereRemovedRestorer.BuildPlacedGuards | Takes the step's own input and plans records and the after-removal world, and gives the step's placed guards; the domain types sit inside them.
NavmeshMember.PreferredOf | Lists the Preferred triangles of a navmesh shape of the collect step's navmesh facts (a tool type).
TerrainGuards.LowestOf | The lowest known height under an object's grid points, over the height lookups the guard is given (tool types only).
WaterLine.LevelOf | Reads a cell's water level from the collect step's water facts (a tool type) by the game's rule: no flag no water, else its own height or the worldspace default.
